## Key Decisions

1. **5 products = 5 asset types, not 5 topic-bundles × 5 assets.** Kimi's blueprint structure mapped 1:1 to the source-of-truth page. $37 tripwire per product, with $148 bundle as the upsell. $37 is a clean per-product price; bundling 5 assets under $37 undercuts the upsell.

2. **No "AI" in marketing copy.** The source-of-truth page was explicit: "Remove the AI from marketing → Sell the Relief of the Result → Avoid triggering Prospect Anxiety." Every Gumroad listing, Reddit thread, and Twitter thread was written to that rule. Zero mentions of "AI-powered" or "automation" in any marketing copy.

3. **The exact hook and guarantee from the source-of-truth page were used verbatim.** Hook: "Instantly eliminate the five most exhausting bottlenecks draining your time and profit with proven, ready-to-deploy systems that do the heavy lifting for you." Guarantee: "If it doesn't save you at least 3 hours of grueling work the very first time you use it... I will refund your $37 in full and you keep everything." No paraphrasing. No "we made it our own." The page is the source.

4. **Marcus persona locked.** All examples, prompts, and positioning default to "Marcus" — the 3-person B2B SaaS content agency owner earning $38k/month, 60+ hour weeks, drowning in operational drag. Specificity converts. The 3 worked examples per product (B2B SaaS, e-commerce, consulting) give a broader audience a hook.

5. **Burner Asset / Schadenfreude / Unearned Efficiency voice.** The source-of-truth page mandated this frame: "Stop teaching people how to fish. Give them a pre-cooked filet on a silver platter. Abandon 'Brand Building' for 'Digital Vending Machines.'" The marketing copy leaned hard into "look like you spent 4 hours on this — you spent 8 minutes."

6. **Closure protocol per `00_Ethos.md`.** Four docs: REPORT, DECISIONS, RISKS, NEXT_3_ACTIONS. The protocol is Gabriel's comprehension framework. Skipping it would have made the work illegible to him.
