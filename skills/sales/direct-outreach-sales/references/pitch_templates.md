```text
You are asking the exact right question. Churn is the silent killer of newsletters, and almost all public advice completely ignores it because "getting 10k subscribers" sounds sexier than "keeping them."

Retention is actually won or lost in the first 14 days of a subscriber signing up.
```

### 2. Large Audience / Zero Conversion Focus
```text
Your points on list conversion are completely correct, but there's a deeper structural reason lists die: the friction of "Cognitive Load vs. Information Density."

Most marketers write emails like essays. They fill them with multiple links, long paragraphs, and secondary thoughts. This creates high cognitive load. When a subscriber opens your email, their brain has to work to find the point. They close it, and they never click.

High-converting list monetization relies on a highly structured, binary direct-response loop:
1. One single topic.
2. One single micro-problem exposed.
3. One single clear action (click or don't click).
```
