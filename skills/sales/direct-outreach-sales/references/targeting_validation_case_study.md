# Targeting Validation — What Gabriel Caught

## Source Session
May 29, 2026 — Gabriel manually reviewed the DeepAgent-generated prospect list and immediately identified why it was wrong.

## What He Caught

### Meenakshi Rohatgi
- LinkedIn profile: "Storytelling | Brand & Content Strategy | Communications"
- 500+ connections, institutional affiliations (Sona Group, Mudra Institute)
- Gabriel's reaction: "Will not be interested in a freaking 9 dollars prompt pack"
- **Why she's not a buyer:** She's a strategist/consultant. She sells brand strategy to organizations. She doesn't need prompts — she writes the briefs that prompts would execute.

### X Woman with 1 Like, 9 Views
- Gabriel's reaction: "She has no positioning. 9 views."
- **Why she's not a buyer:** No audience, no engagement. Even if she bought, she's not the beachhead. The point isn't to sell to someone with 9 views — it's to sell to people WHERE people are already looking.

## The Pattern

**DeepAgent scraped for keywords** ("newsletter not growing," "email list not converting") without checking:
1. Is the person posting the complaint actually the END USER?
2. Or are they a STRATEGIST/TEACHER who talks ABOUT the problem?
3. Do they have any positioning/authority/audience?

**Gabriel's rule:** "Never start something without foundations. Some authority, some audience."

He's been in this world for too long. He knows that cold outreach without positioning is throwing spaghetti. His instinct was right. The agent should have caught this before drafting messages.

## Buyer Profile (Confirmed)

**Actual buyers for $9-$29 prompt packs:**
- Solo creators doing ALL their own content
- Freelance social media managers producing for clients
- Small business owners who "know they need email marketing" but can't afford help
- Newsletter operators stuck at a plateau (have list, can't grow/monetize)
- People who complain about CONTENT PRODUCTION being the bottleneck

**Not buyers:**
- Strategists, consultants, agency owners
- People selling courses ABOUT the problem
- Anyone with institutional backing
- Anyone who teaches for a living
- Anyone whose profile says "Brand Strategy" or "Communications"
